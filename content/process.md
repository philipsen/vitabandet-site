The route is a personal GPX, maintained in BaseCamp, along the White Ribbon corridor (pass **väster om** the six official waypoints). An **alt ~25 km** schedule is the public plan of record.

## Pacing

Day 1 is short (**15 km**), day 2 **20.5 km**, then about **25 km** on ski days. Mean ski day after the easy start is about **22 km**. Elevation along the line is a rough DEM guide, not a promise.

## Rest vs one night

Two nights (arrive, shop, rest) at **Storlien, Valsjöbua, Gäddede, Klimpfjäll, Ritsem, Abisko**.

**Hemavan** and **Jäckvik** are one overnight plus a few hours to resupply — no extra rest day. Dropping the Hemavan rest pulled every later date forward by one day and finishes the trip on **15 April**.

## Companions

- **Grövelsjön → Storlien:** shared tent; they take the train to Mora and drive the car home.
- **Hemavan → Kvikkjokk:** Klaartje meets on the Hemavan night (**15 Mar**), skis with me to Kvikkjokk (**25 Mar**), bus 47 out (weekday morning). Train and bus only — not the Mora car.

## What this site does not include

Fuel strategy, buy lists, pulk weights, and unnamed wild-camp coordinates stay in the private planning repo. Named villages and STF huts on the itinerary are fair to publish.
