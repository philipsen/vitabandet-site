The route is a personal GPX, maintained in BaseCamp, along the White Ribbon corridor (pass **väster om** the six official waypoints). The public plan is the [itinerary](/itinerary/): **5 Feb – 18 Apr 2027**, about **1,296 km**, **73 days**.

## Pacing

Ski days are mostly **11–29 km**. Elevation along the line is a rough DEM guide, not a promise.

## Rest vs one night

Days with no ski: **Storlien** (14 Feb), **Gäddede** (1 Mar), **Hemavan** (13 Mar), **Kvikkjokk** (27 Mar), **Ritsem** (3 Apr). Abisko is one night.

## Companions

- **Grövelsjön → Storlien:** shared tent. They stop in Storlien on **13 Feb**.
- **Hemavan → Kvikkjokk:** Klaartje meets on the Hemavan shop night (**12 Mar**), rest **13 Mar**, and the Kvikkjokk shop day is **26 Mar**.

## What this site does not include

Fuel strategy, buy lists, pulk weights, and unnamed wild-camp coordinates stay in the private planning repo. Named villages and STF huts on the itinerary are fair to publish.
