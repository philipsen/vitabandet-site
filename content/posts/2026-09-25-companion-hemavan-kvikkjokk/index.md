This stretch is about twelve ski days after Hemavan, or five if you stop at Ammarnäs. I am already in Hemavan on **Saturday 13 March**. You take the early bus on **Sunday 14 March** and we rest that day. We ski out on **Monday 15 March**.

The huts run Hemavan, Viterskalet, Syter, Tärnasjö, Serve, then Ammarnäs. After that it is tent, with a night in Adolfström. Pieljekaisestugan is a stop on the way to Jäckvik, not a night.

**Sunday 14 March** the early bus is in Hemavan about **13:05** (Umeå **07:30**). That is a tight connection off the night train. We do not ski that afternoon.

Serve is a short day, **11 km**. Ammarnäs is the next night. If you stop there, the morning bus is **Saturday 20 March**.

Kvikkjokk is **Saturday 27 March**, påskafton. There is no bus out on 27–29 March. The first bus is **Tuesday 30 March**, about **05:25**.
