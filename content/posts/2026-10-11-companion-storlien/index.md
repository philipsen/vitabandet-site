This is the opening leg if you want about nine ski days, then a train. We start at **Grövelsjön** and ski to **Storlien**. You stop there. I rest a day and continue north.

Own pulk, own food. We share one tent and one stove. Helags, Sylarna, and Blåhammaren are closed until 5 March, so the alpine nights are tent.

Working dates from a **5 February** start. They will slip with weather. Days **1–9** are the trip together. Days **10–14** are the next stretch, so you can see what is ahead if you stay on past Storlien.

Day 5 is the climb past Svalåtjärn. Days 7 and 8 are the alpine stretch, west of the closed stations. Day 9 is the long drop into Storlien (↓804 m), past Blåhammaren with no camp there. **Hävlingestugorna** is a Länsstyrelsen cabin (book it). **Broktjärnskojan** is an unlocked rest hut. **Enkälen** is emergency rest only, so the night is in the tent beside it. Rogen’s STF hut opens 19 February, after we have passed.

Day 12’s camp is on the line near **Skalstugan**, not at the Anjan bus stop. **Kolåsen** itself is the day after this table.

## Out from Storlien

This is the plan. Night in town (STF Storliens fjällgård or Högfjällshotell). Morning train toward **Östersund**.

On the Norrtåg timetable for **10 August – 12 December 2026**, weekday and weekend trains leave Storlien at **09:27** and **18:37**. The 09:27 is in **Östersund C at 11:27**. The national timetable changes in mid-December, so February 2027 will not be this sheet. Check [Norrtåg](https://norrtag.se/tidtabeller/) and [SJ](https://www.sj.se/) before you book. Skis and a packed pulk are luggage; ask when you buy the ticket.

## If you come out at Anjan

**Anjan** is a stop on road 336, west of Kall, on [Länstrafiken](https://ltr.se/) bus **551** (Kallsedet – Kall – Järpen). The 551 does not run Saturday or Sunday. The outer stops, Anjan included, are often **only if you book**. Call **0771-820 083** by **16:00 the weekday before**.

On the 2025 timetables (28 April – 20 August) the morning bus leaves Anjan about **06:20** and is at **Järpen biblioteket at 08:00**. I have not seen an evening bus from Anjan back toward Järpen on those sheets. Treat 06:20 as the pattern, not a February promise. Confirm on [ltr.se](https://ltr.se/).

From Järpen to Östersund, same Norrtåg period, weekdays toward Östersund C:

| Järpen | Östersund C |
|--------|-------------|
| 06:27 | 07:21 |
| 10:32 | 11:27 |
| 12:30 | 13:25 |
| 15:31 | 16:25 |
| 19:39 | 20:35 |

The 06:27 leaves before the morning bus arrives. The connection after an 08:00 arrival in Järpen is the **10:32**. Saturday adds an 08:33 from Järpen, but 551 does not run then. Bus **156** also runs Järpen – Östersund and may beat the train; check it on the same site. Skis go in the luggage hold if there is room. The driver decides. No fuel bottles on the bus.

## If you come out at Kolåsen

**Kolåsen** is the same bus, further out the road, and it is thinner. Most days 551 ends at **Kallsedet**, about **10 km** before the hotel. To be picked up at Kolåsen itself you book the **T** trip on 551: same number, **0771-820 083**, by 16:00 the weekday before. [Kolåsens Fjällhotell](https://kolasen.se/en/getting-here/) describes the same bus, and they will fetch you from Kallsedet if you ask them when you book a bed.

On the spring 2025 sheet the booked trips left the Kolåsen stops late morning and mid-afternoon and reached Järpen about **14:40** and **17:15**. From there the **15:31** or **19:39** train is the match, if those times still exist. Kallsedet, without the extra booking, had a weekday **06:45 → Järpen 08:00** and an afternoon bus around **17:00**.

There is no weekend 551. If you arrive at the road on a Saturday, you are waiting until Monday or calling a taxi from Järpen or Åre.

The day named Kolåsen in the full plan is the camp at Kallsedet, not the hotel. The hotel is the detour, and the bus stop you can count on without a phone call.
