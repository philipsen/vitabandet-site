Planning the route is a large part of the fun.

Over the years I’ve done several parts of the line, especially in the north. I’ve been in the south too, but that was so long ago I don’t remember exactly where. I’m pretty sure I did part of Jämtland.

For Vita Bandet there is no fixed route. The only requirement is to stay west of a handful of waypoints, so you stay in the mountains. You can keep to Sweden or cross into Norway.

I started by looking at a lot of tracks from people who have already done the trip. Most follow more or less the same line. The big choice in the south is Storlien (in red) or Undersåker (in blue). The undersaker route id shorter, but seems to have more roads, Storlien is more in the mountains but a bit longer. My preference will be Storlien, depending on conditions while underway.

{{< gpx-map file="helags-via-storlien.gpx" file2="helags-via-undersaker.gpx" default-layer="winter" >}}

After that, most people follow more or less the same route to Hemavan. From there, most people take Kungsleden all the way up to Abisko. From Abisko the usual choice is to cross Torneträsk and then stay on the Swedish side all the way to Treriksröset.

In the north I pick Lapplandsleden from Klimpfjäll to Hemavan. This stays more in the mountains.

Then I want Padjelanta rather than Kungsleden all the way. kunhsleden is busy. saltaluokta is very, had bad conditions 2 years agoo. Stretch from saltaluk back to kungs leden is along a road of often upwind on a lake. I have done padjalanta in 2024. It was very nice. Then from ritsum i'll go north and then east back to kungsleden.

From Abisko I follow Nordkalottleden through Norway to the finish. The stand seems very flat. I have done Nordkatol in the past, very late in the season (early May of 2013). It is a great trek.
