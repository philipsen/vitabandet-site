Planning the route is a large part of the fun.

Over the years I’ve done several parts of the route, especially in the north. I’ve been in the south too, but that was so long ago I don’t remember exactly where. I’m pretty sure I did part of Jämtland.

For Vita Bandet there is no fixed route. The only requirement is to stay west of a handful of waypoints, so you stay in the mountains. You can keep to Sweden or cross into Norway.

I started by looking at a lot of tracks from people who have already done the trip. Most follow more or less the same track. The big choice in the south is Storlien (in red) or Undersåker (in blue). The undersaker route id shorter, but seems to have more roads, Storlien is more in the mountains but a bit longer. My preference will be Storlien, depending on conditions while underway.

After that, most people follow more or less the same route to Hemavan, and then Kungsleden all the way up to Abisko.

In the north I prefer to try to tkae Lapplandsleden from Klimpfjäll to Hemavan. This stays more in the mountains.

Later I plan to take the Padjelanta trail rather than Kungsleden all the way. Kungsleden is busy. Saltaluokta is a great hut, but it is quite low in elevation and I had very bad conditions there 2 years ago. The stretch from Saltaluokta back west to Kungsleden is along a road of often upwind on a lake. I have done Padjalanta in 2024. It was very nice. Then from Ritsum I'll go north and then east back to kungsleden.

From Abisko the usual choice is to cross Torneträsk and then stay on the Swedish side all the way to Treriksröset. I plan to follow Nordkalottleden through Norway to the finish. The standard seems very flat. I have done Nordkalottleden in the past, very late in the season (early May of 2013). It is a great trek.
