This stretch is a good one if someone wants a ski trip of a week or so . [Lapplandsleden](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/vasterbotten/besoksmal/vandringsleder/lapplandsleden.html) is the marked winter trail from Borgafjäll toward Hemavan (red crosses). I wrote about why I prefer it [here](/posts/2026-09-23-lapplandsleden-nordkalottleden/).

Two join points: **Gäddede** (longer) or **Klimpfjäll** (shorter). Both end in **Hemavan**, which has a proper bus and a tiny airport. There is **no winter bus** Gäddede → Klimpfjäll — Vildmarksvägen / Stekenjokk is closed.

Working dates from a **5 February** start. They will slip with weather.

| | Gäddede start | Klimpfjäll start |
|--|----------------|------------------|
| Meet | **28 Feb–1 Mar** (shop + rest) | **5 Mar** (shop, then ski) |
| Ski out | 2 Mar | 6 Mar |
| Hemavan | **12 Mar** · rest **13 Mar** | **12 Mar** · rest **13 Mar** |
| Ski days | 11 | 7 |
| Food | ICA Gäddede · top-up Klimpfjäll Handlar'n | Handlar'n on arrival |

Own pulk. We share tent and stove for the tent nights. At the four Länsstyrelsen huts we sleep inside. I continue north from Hemavan; Klaartje will join there.

The line is Gäddede to Hemavan. Up to Klimpfjäll it is the track in from Gäddede; from there it is the official winter trail. The Borgafjäll start is not on this map. The first two ski days are tent until Slipsik.

Hut nights are **Slipsik**, **Tjåkkele**, **Åtnik**, and **Arevattnet**. **8 Mar** is the long gap on the kalfjäll, between Åtnik and Arevattnet. **Gränssjöarna** is about a kilometre off that camp if you want a cabin or a shower. I ski into Gäddede on 28 Feb (11 km). If you join there, you can already be in the village.

## Huts — not free, and you still need a tent

Länsstyrelsen has **four** overnight huts on the trail: **Slipsik**, **Tjåkkele**, **Åtnik**, **Arevattnet**. Unlocked. Wood stove, kitchen kit, mattresses, pillows, blankets. Bring a sheet or sleeping bag. **You cannot book.** Hikers and skiers get the beds; if you already slept a night, you offer the bunk to the next person.

**200 kr per person per night** if you sleep inside (children 0–15 free). Pay afterwards — Swish or transfer, details in the hut. They are **not** STF, and they are **not** free.

We plan to **use them**. The tent is for the nights without a hut, and as backup if a hut is full or too cold. Länsstyrelsen is clear: the gaps are long, so a multi-day trip still needs a tent. Rastskydd along the way are for weather, not planned nights.

## Nights at the huts

The days land on the hut line. Sleep **inside** at **Slipsik**, **Tjåkkele**, **Åtnik**, and **Arevattnet**. [Gränssjöarna](https://granssjoarna.se/) is about a kilometre off the 8 Mar camp (optional cabin / shower / sauna, phone first in March).

Gäddede → Slipsik is **tent on the track** (2 and 3 Mar). After Arevattnet: tent at **Atostugan** (parking/rastplats at Atoklimpen, not one of the four official overnight huts), then **Goeblejaevrie**, then Hemavan. Borgafjäll hotel is about 24 km **east** — a storm detour, not the line.

## Getting to Gäddede

Thin. **Länstrafiken Jämtland 425** is the last bus: **Strömsund → Gäddede**, about **2 h**.

Winter pattern now (check [ltr.se](https://ltr.se/) / Resrobot before you book — March 2027 will move):

| | Strömsund → Gäddede | Gäddede → Strömsund |
|--|---------------------|---------------------|
| Mon–Fri | **15:25 → ~17:25** | **06:05 → ~08:05** |
| Sat | **no bus** | **no bus** |
| Sun | **19:10** toward Gäddede | **16:45** toward Strömsund |

To hit **15:25** in Strömsund: train to **Östersund**, then **bus 142** Östersund → Strömsund (~1¼ h, several weekday runs). From the south that is a **morning train to Östersund**, bus, then 425. The useful arrival is **Fri 26 Feb ~17:25**, so you have Saturday in Gäddede before the shop day. Shop is **Sunday 28 Feb**, rest **Monday 1 Mar**, ski out **Tuesday 2 Mar**. Stay at [Pilgrimshotellet](https://www.pilgrimshotellet.se/). Gäddede Camping is open year-round if you want a pitch. The Sunday 19:10 bus lands late on the shop day.

Skis: packed, in the luggage space if there is room — the driver decides.

## Getting to Klimpfjäll

Better hub. **Länstrafiken Västerbotten 420** **Vilhelmina → Klimpfjäll**, about **1 h 50**, via Saxnäs. [tabussen.nu](https://www.tabussen.nu/).

Typical winter pattern:

| | Toward Klimpfjäll | Toward Vilhelmina |
|--|-------------------|-------------------|
| Mon–Fri | about **11:10** and **16:20** from Vilhelmina (arrive ~12:50 / ~18:25) | from Klimpfjäll about **06:00** and **13:55** |
| Sat | **no 420** | **no 420** |
| Sun | **18:35** from Vilhelmina | check the PDF |

**5 Mar 2027 is a Friday**, so the weekday 420 can land you that afternoon. I ski into Klimpfjäll the same day. We leave **Saturday 6 Mar**. There is no rest day in the village. Vilhelmina itself is on inland buses toward Umeå / Lycksele / Östersund, and has a small airport.

## Home from Hemavan

This is the easy end.

**Bus 31** Hemavan ↔ Umeå, daily, about **5–6 h**. Weekdays there are a few runs (Umeå toward Hemavan around **07:00**, **14:10**, **16:10**). Weekends are thinner. From Hemavan there is a morning bus toward Umeå. Timetable changed **2 Feb 2026** — use the current PDF on [tabussen.nu](https://www.tabussen.nu/). Then SJ / Norrtåg south from Umeå.

**Plane:** [Hemavan Tärnaby Airport](https://www.hemavantarnabyairport.se/) is in the village. **PopulAir** Arlanda ↔ Hemavan, about **six days a week** in winter (often **no Saturday**). March is high season on the state route, often two weekday round trips. Times are not up for March 2027 yet; last winter a typical pattern was **Arlanda ~08:50 → Hemavan late morning**, and an **afternoon** flight back. Book [populair.com](https://www.populair.com/). Local **320** covers Hemavan–Tärnaby.

**12 Mar is a Friday**. I arrive that afternoon and shop. The rest day is **Saturday 13 Mar**. You can leave Saturday or Sunday. A Friday flight the afternoon we arrive is a tight connection off the trail.

If the ski is late, wait in Hemavan. Do not plan a tight same-day connection off the trail.

Times above are the **2026/27 winter** pattern. Recheck [Resrobot](https://www.resrobot.se/), [ltr.se](https://ltr.se/) and [tabussen.nu](https://www.tabussen.nu/) when the winter 2027 books open.
