A winter journey on [Fjällfararnas Vita Bandet](https://www.vitagronabandet.se/en/white-green-ribbon/) — the White Ribbon — south to north along Sweden’s mountain chain.

I've been doing winter cross-country ski trips for a lot of years. They've been getting longer. A long time ago, we used to do a lot of camping. Recent years, that hasn't happened as much.

**~1,299 km** · **65 days** · **6 rest nights** · ski and pulk · mostly tent.

This site is the public notebook: how the plan was built, the summarised itinerary, and a daily log from the trail. The planning dump (fuel, packing, full kilometre tables) stays private.

Start **10 February 2027** · finish **15 April 2027** at Treriksröset.
