A winter journey on [Fjällfararnas Vita Bandet](https://www.vitagronabandet.se/en/white-green-ribbon/) — the White Ribbon — south to north along Sweden’s mountain chain.

{{< ski-rotator >}}

## Why this trip

I've been doing winter cross-country ski trips for a lot of years. Almost every winter there's a trip in Norway or Sweden.

A few years ago I met some people doing something called Vita Bandet. I had never heard of it. It sounded really cool: ski the Swedish mountains end to end, usually south to north. It takes a lot of time.

I've been thinking about doing the trip for a couple of years. Next season — 2027 — is the year to do it.
