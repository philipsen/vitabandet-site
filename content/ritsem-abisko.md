This is the hut stretch: **Ritsem → Abisko**, about **121 km**. Be at Ritsem on **Saturday 3 April**. You are two. **Sunday 4 April** is spare if weather delays the approach. The ski north is **5–11 April**. Abisko is one night.

Most Vita Bandet tracks follow Kungsleden all the way from Hemavan. I come in from Padjelanta instead, rest at Ritsem, then ski north of the lake and east onto Kungsleden, staying west of Nikkaluokta.

That keeps me off the busy Kebnekaise corridor. Nights sit at staffed STF huts: Sitojaure, Hukejaure (Nordkalottleden, west of Nikkaluokta), then Sälka, Tjäktja, Alesjaure, Abiskojaure.

## Days

From Sitojaure to Abisko the nights are in staffed STF huts. The approach to Ritsem is tent. Beds have **mattresses and duvets**; bring a sheet (or a liner) to cover them. Each hut has a **kitchen for guest use**. Hut shops sell some food — snacks, staples, freeze-dried when they have it — not a cooked meal. Ritsem has a café and a proper shop; Abisko has a restaurant and a grocery in the village.

From Sälka north it is classic Kungsleden: Tjäktja pass, then down through the lakes to Abisko. Tjäktja is short but steep with a pulk.

I am still on the approach on **3 April** (tent). You are already at the station. If that day runs late, **4 April** is the spare, and the hut nights below stay where they are. When the approach is on time, **4 April** is the shop and the rest, and we ski out on **5 April**.

Book **six nights, two beds**, and only the mountain huts. Leave **Ritsem** and **Abisko turiststation** out of this booking. ## Bail-out

Two ways off before Abisko.

**From Hukejaure, go back to Ritsem.** Reverse the first two days: Hukejaure → Sitojaure (19 km) → Ritsem (22 km). Same staffed huts. Then the Falcks bus to Gällivare, the way you came in. That is the clean early exit, before Tjäktja.

**Later, east to Singi and Kebnekaise.** From Sälka the valleys split. Ski to **STF Singi** (~12 km), then **STF Kebnekaise** (~14 km). Kebnekaise is open **26 Feb – 18 Apr 2027**. From there it is about 19 km down to **Nikkaluokta**, or a booked snowmobile. The road starts at Nikkaluokta: [Nikkaluoktaexpressen](https://nikkaluoktaexpressen.se/) to Kiruna while the mountain station is open, then the train. This is the busy corridor we otherwise stay west of.

Once over Tjäktja, Abisko is usually the simpler way out. Laponia still wants rented kit at Gällivare station — from Nikkaluokta that is Kiruna, then a short train south.

## Travel

This is the easiest public-transport gate on the trip. The likely way in is the night train from **Stockholm** to **Gällivare**, then the bus to Ritsem. Out is the train at Abisko.

**In.** Night train **Stockholm → Gällivare** (SJ; for 2027 the Stockholm–Narvik night train is planned to run through again). Then **Falcks Omnibus** to **Ritsem Fjällstation** — typically one or two buses a day in late winter, about 3½ hours. Book the bus **on the same ticket as the train** (Resplus, destination *Ritsem Fjällstation*) so a late train is the operator’s problem, not an unplanned night in town. Timetable and tickets: [roadtoritsem.com](https://roadtoritsem.com/). STF’s page: [transport to Ritsem](https://www.swedishtouristassociation.com/facilities/stf-ritsem/transportation/).

Be at **STF Ritsem on Saturday 3 April**. On the current winter sheet the bus leaves **Gällivare Resecentrum at 09:00** and is at **Ritsem at 12:35**. April 2027 is not published yet. A night train that arrives around 08:00 can feed that bus. Friday night in Gällivare, then the Saturday bus, is the calm version. Falcks: **0970-248 00**. Laponia, if you are collecting rented kit, wants 15–30 minutes at the station.

**Out.** Ski into **STF Abisko Turiststation** on **11 April**. The station is on the Malmbanan. Night train south toward Kiruna / Stockholm, or a day train. I leave the next morning on Nordkalottleden toward Treriksröset.

## Packing

Each of you packs this. No tent and no stove. Cook in the hut kitchen. The liner covers the hut bed. Beds already have a mattress and a duvet.

**Documents.** NKBV card, NOK and SEK, wallet, ING credit card, passport, tickets, STF number.

**Ski and sleep.** Skis, boots, gaiters, wax, climbing skins, short skins, poles, headlamp, snow shovel, sleeping pad, bivy sack, summer sleeping bag, liner, lip balm, sunscreen, toothpaste, toothbrush, small towel, pocket knife, tea towel.

**Small kit.** Compass, ski goggles, glacier glasses, lighter, sports tape, interdental brushes, a small bottle of soap, maps, maps in the GPS, first aid kit, blue thermos, mug, spoon.

**Food, each person.** Muesli 1 kg, milk 4 packs, cup-a-soup 20, Snickers or bars 20, granola bars 20, dried fruit 500 g, nuts 1 kg, hydration tablets 1 tube, coffee.

**Day clothes.** Icebreaker undershirt, thicker merino shirt, thin fleece, balaclava, anorak, Fjällräven pants, rain pants, thin gloves, red gloves, one thin pair of socks, one thick pair, down jacket.

**Evening clothes.** IB 200 long-sleeve, underwear, down booties, socks, long johns, thin fleece, white gloves.

## Renting kit

**[Laponia Adventures](https://laponiaadventures.com/outdoor-equipment/)** rents Åsnes backcountry skis with NNN BC bindings, Lundhags ski boots, poles, skins, and a **Jemtlander 14** pulk, plus a tent, winter sleeping bag, mat, stove, and winter clothes. Book online at least seven days ahead. For two people, email [info@laponiaadventures.com](mailto:info@laponiaadventures.com).

In winter they **deliver to and collect from Gällivare train station** (extra fee). Collect the kit on arrival, then take the Ritsem bus. After Abisko, the train south is about two hours to Gällivare. Hand the kit back at the station. Shop-to-shop one-way is Jokkmokk ↔ Gällivare, not Abisko — ask them to write the station loop into the booking.

Own skis can be sent ahead to STF Ritsem or Gällivare and shipped home from Abisko (Fjällboden handles parcels).
